Volunteer Guide
How to register, participate & get your certificate
Five Simple Steps
1

🌐 Register on JanPrayas

Open the JanPrayas portal → Volunteer section → fill your name, mobile number, area/gram panchayat, profession and skills. Free, takes under 2 minutes.

2

🔍 Browse Open Missions

See live missions on the home page — tree plantations, cleanliness drives, health camps, education sessions. Filter by location or date.

3

✋ Register for a Mission

Click "Register" on any open mission. Enter your name and mobile number. You can also float your own community mission from "Host Mission".

4

📲 Check In at Venue with Selfie

Reach the venue on time. The organiser shows a QR code. Scan it → allow camera → take a live selfie. This is your official attendance record — no fake check-ins possible.

5

📜 Download Your Certificate

After mission completes → visit "My Volunteer Passport" (enter name + mobile) → download official participation certificate and ID card. Share it, frame it, add it to your CV.
