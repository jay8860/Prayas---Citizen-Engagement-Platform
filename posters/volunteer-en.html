How to Become a JanPrayas Volunteer
Free to join · Open to every citizen of Dhamtari
1

🌐 Register on JanPrayas

Visit janprayas.dhamtari.gov → scroll to the Volunteer section → fill your full name, mobile number, area / gram panchayat, profession, and skills. Registration is free and takes under 2 minutes.

2

🔍 Browse Open Missions

See live missions on the home page — tree plantations, cleanliness drives, health camps, education sessions, and more. Filter by location or date to find one near you.

3

✋ Register for a Mission

Click "Register" on any open mission card. Enter your name and mobile number. You'll receive a confirmation. You can also float your own community mission from the "Host Mission" section.

4

📲 Check In at the Venue

On the day, reach the venue on time. The organiser will display a QR code. Scan it with your mobile browser → allow camera → take a live selfie to confirm you are present. This is your official attendance record.

5

📜 Download Your Certificate

After the mission is marked complete, visit My Volunteer Passport (enter your name + mobile) → download your official participation certificate and ID card. Share it, frame it, or add it to your CV.
