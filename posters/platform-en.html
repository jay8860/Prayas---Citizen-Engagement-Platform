How JanPrayas Works
From a mission idea to official recognition
Step by Step
1
📋

Mission is Created

Government officials, verified NGOs, schools, or registered citizens host missions — tree plantations, cleanliness drives, health camps, awareness rallies — on the JanPrayas platform.

2
🙋

Volunteers Register

Any citizen of Dhamtari can register free. Browse open missions, choose what suits your skills and schedule, and sign up with one tap.

3
📲

Attend & Check In at Venue

Show up on the day. The organiser displays a QR code at the venue. Scan it on your phone and take a live selfie — your attendance is officially recorded.

4
🏅

Earn Official Recognition

Download your participation certificate. Accumulate points, earn badges, rise on the district leaderboard, and qualify for the JanPrayas Samman — Dhamtari's civic honour.
