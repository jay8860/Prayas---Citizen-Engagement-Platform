How JanPrayas Works
From a mission idea to official recognition — four simple steps
1
📋

Mission is Created

Government officials, verified NGOs, schools, or registered citizens host missions — tree plantations, cleanliness drives, health camps, awareness rallies, and more — on the JanPrayas platform.

2
🙋

Volunteers Register

Any citizen of Dhamtari can register free on JanPrayas. Browse open missions, choose what suits your skills and schedule, and sign up with one tap.

3
📲

Attend & Check In

Show up at the venue on the day. The organiser displays a QR code. Scan it on your phone and take a live selfie — this confirms your physical presence and marks your attendance.

4
🏅

Earn Recognition

Download your official participation certificate. Accumulate points, earn badges, rise on the district leaderboard, and qualify for the JanPrayas Samman — district-level civic honour.
