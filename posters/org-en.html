Six Steps to Get Started
1

🏢 Register Your Organization

Open JanPrayas portal → Organizations section → "Register / Log In" → fill org name, type, description and contact. Submit to create your account.

2

⏳ Await Admin Approval 1–3 days

District admin reviews your registration. Approved orgs get a verified badge. Your missions then publish instantly — no per-mission approval needed.

3

📋 Post a Mission

Log in → Missions tab → fill title, category, date, location, volunteer slots. Your org name auto-fills. Citizens across Dhamtari see and register for it.

4

📲 QR Check-In at Venue

Display the QR code (from your mission card) at the venue on the day. Volunteers scan it and take a live selfie — attendance is verified, no proxies possible.

5

📸 Upload Photos & Mark Complete

After the drive → mark "Completed" → add turnout count, outcome note and up to 8 photos. This triggers certificate eligibility for all checked-in volunteers.

6

🏆 Earn Points & District Recognition

20 base points per completed mission + 5 points per verified volunteer. High-scoring orgs rise on the leaderboard and appear in the district showcase.

📜
Volunteer Certificates
Every checked-in volunteer can download an official participation certificate from their Volunteer Passport.
🌟
Org Recognition
District leaderboard ranking, verified showcase listing, and JanPrayas Samman eligibility for your org.
💡 Tip: Keep your profile complete — description, logo and contact details. Citizens and admins trust well-described, active organizations. Consistent missions = more points, wider reach.
