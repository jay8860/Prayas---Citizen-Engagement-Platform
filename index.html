📖 Getting Started — How the platform works click to expand
1
Register & get approved
Submit your organization's profile. A district admin reviews it — you'll receive approval status here. Approved orgs get a green badge and their missions are auto-published without waiting for admin review.
2
Post a Mission
Go to the Missions tab → fill in the title, category, date, location, and volunteer slots. Your org name is pre-filled automatically. Approved orgs: missions appear on the public board instantly. Pending orgs: admin reviews first.
3
Volunteers register & show up
Citizens browse missions and register. On the day, the QR check-in code appears on the mission card (visible to you in the admin view after coordinator logs in). Volunteers scan it at the venue — they must take a live selfie to verify physical presence.
4
Mark the mission completed
After the drive, mark the mission complete and add the actual turnout count + a short outcome note. You can upload up to 8 photos. This triggers certificate eligibility for volunteers who checked in.
5
Certificates & Points
Volunteers: Each volunteer who checked in can download a participation certificate from their Volunteer Passport.
Your org: Earns 20 base points per mission + 5 points per verified check-in. Consistent monthly activity earns bonus points. View your rank on the Points & Rank tab. High-scoring orgs appear in the district showcase.
💡 Tip: Keep your org profile complete (description, logo, contact) — volunteers trust and choose active, verified organizations with clear information.

📋 How Volunteer Check-in & Certificates Work

Volunteers earn a participation certificate only when both of the following conditions are met:

1
Volunteer must be registered on JanPrayas.
They must have signed up at this portal using their mobile number before your event. Unregistered walk-ins cannot check in or receive certificates.
2
Volunteer must scan the QR code at the venue on the day of the mission.
Display your mission's QR check-in poster at the event venue. Each volunteer opens JanPrayas on their phone, scans the QR code, and takes a live selfie to confirm physical presence. This selfie is stored as proof of attendance.
💡 Tip for your event: Print and display the Check-in Poster (available below for each approved mission) at a clearly visible spot at your venue. Ask volunteers to scan it as they arrive. After the event, mark the mission as Completed — certificates are then automatically generated for every volunteer who checked in.
✅ Walk-in / Self-Reporting Option (Recommended for new events): If a volunteer arrives without having pre-registered, your organization's login can mark their attendance on the spot using the "Mark Walk-in Attendance" form on each mission card below. Just enter their name and mobile number — they will be auto-registered on the portal instantly, and can fill in their full profile details later. They will still be eligible for a participation certificate once the mission is marked completed.

Your organization earns 20 base points per mission + 5 points per QR-verified or walk-in volunteer. Consistent monthly activity earns bonus points.

JanPrayas Samman — every rank is honoured
